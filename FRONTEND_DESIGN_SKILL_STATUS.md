### What is Frontend-Design Skill?

The **frontend-design** skill is a specialized Claude plugin for creating distinctive, production-grade frontend interfaces with high design quality. It's designed to:

1. **Create Visual Excellence**
   - Help build web components and pages with striking designs
   - Avoid generic AI aesthetics ("AI slop")
   - Create memorable, cohesive interfaces

2. **Design Thinking Approach**
   Before coding, it emphasizes understanding:
   - **Purpose**: What problem does it solve? Who uses it?
   - **Tone**: Bold aesthetic direction (minimalist, maximalist, retro, luxury, brutalist, etc.)
   - **Constraints**: Technical requirements and framework limitations
   - **Differentiation**: What makes it unforgettable?

3. **Key Design Focus Areas**
   - **Typography**: Distinctive, characterful fonts
   - **Color & Theme**: Cohesive palettes with dominant colors and accents
   - **Motion**: Strategic animations with high-impact moments
   - **Spatial Composition**: Unexpected layouts with asymmetry and overlap
   - **Backgrounds & Details**: Atmospheric effects, textures, gradients

4. **What It Avoids**
   - Generic AI aesthetics
   - Overused fonts (Inter, Roboto, Arial)
   - Clichéd color schemes (purple gradients)
   - Predictable cookie-cutter patterns
